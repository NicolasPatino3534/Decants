# Product

## Register

product

## Users

DecantsCBA has two primary user groups: perfume buyers browsing and buying decants from Argentina, and the store owner/operator managing orders and catalog data. The public shopper wants to discover fragrances safely without buying a full bottle blind. The operator needs a simple, low-friction admin surface for reviewing orders, editing products, and checking basic income balance.

## Product Purpose

DecantsCBA is a full-stack ecommerce site for selling perfume decants in 2ml, 5ml, and 10ml formats. Success means shoppers can evaluate products, add variants to cart, complete checkout, and receive clear follow-up, while the owner can keep the catalog accurate and understand sales without navigating a complex back office.

## Brand Personality

Premium, clean, elegant, modern, and minimal. The experience should feel boutique and trustworthy, with restrained white surfaces and refined gold accents rather than saturated or flashy luxury cues.

## Anti-references

Avoid a dark-heavy luxury look, cheap saturated gold, cluttered dashboards, marketing-style admin pages, unnecessary sections such as users/roles/settings/analytics, and decorative UI that slows down routine operations.

## Design Principles

1. Keep operational surfaces simple enough for repeated daily use.
2. Make trust visible through clear pricing, stock, status, contact, and fulfillment details.
3. Use gold as an accent for hierarchy and action, not as decoration everywhere.
4. Prefer direct editing and readable detail views over complex dashboards.
5. Treat the current login-free admin as temporary and production-unsafe until authentication is restored.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible controls, readable Spanish copy, visible focus states, and responsive layouts that remain usable on mobile. Motion should be minimal and respect reduced-motion preferences.
